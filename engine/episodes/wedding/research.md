# Xu Chenghong, the stranger wedding (Yunnan, Oct 2026): verified facts

## Background (Yahoo / Malaysia)
- Father died when he was 3. His mother remarried 2 years later.
- He and his older brother were raised by their grandmother, who died while he was still a child.
- He worked various jobs and saved more than 160,000 yuan to marry his girlfriend, Luo Chui (other reports spell her name "Chuichui").

## The invitation (Yahoo / VnExpress)
- He feared too few relatives and friends would fill even a few banquet tables, so he invited strangers in a Douyin video.
- Some outlets say he hoped for 10–20 people. Not used in the video.

## The crowd (VnExpress)
- Nearly 20,000 people travelled to Luowang Town, Zhaotong City.
- Hotels were 100% occupied on 28–29 Oct and fully booked by 30 Oct.
- Some restaurants reported Lunar-New-Year-level revenue.
- The tourism bureau's deputy director visited the family; the bureau coordinated 11 counties and offered discounted tickets.
- The crowds strained traffic, parking and security.

## Help and gifts (Yahoo)
- Appliances, food, livestock, volunteer chefs, a convoy of wedding cars, and a luxury Maybach for the couple.
- The event moved to a larger public space.
- Authorities handled crowds, traffic and food safety.

## Authenticity
- Douyin reportedly confirmed the account was genuine and removed 100+ fake accounts impersonating him and his relatives (zamin.uz / secondary).

## Date
- Wedding on 30 Oct (VnExpress) or 31 Oct (others). The video says "అక్టోబర్ చివర్లో".

## Not used
- "2 tonnes of chicken" and "50 stations" (unverified).
