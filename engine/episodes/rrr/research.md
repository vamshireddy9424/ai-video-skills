# Hyderabad Regional Ring Road (RRR): research (31 Oct 2026)

## Hook
- **Kokapet, HMDA e-auction, Sept 2026:** Neopolis Plot 17 fetched ₹120 cr/acre; Golden Mile Site-1 fetched ₹115 cr/acre (Telangana Today, 9 Sep 2026).
- **Kokapet, 2021 auction:** peak ₹60.20 cr/acre. So ~2× in about 5 years.
- **Not in the video:** the late-2025 record was ₹151.25 cr/acre (Neopolis), so the hook says "this September", not "a record".

## Project
- **Length:** ~340 km in total. North is 158.6 km (Bharatmala) or 161 km (NHAI tender). The source pages differ.
- **Distance:** roughly 30 km outside the 158 km ORR (themetrorailguy).
- **North towns:** Sangareddy, Narsapur, Toopran, Gajwel, Yadadri, Bhongir, Choutuppal.
- **South towns:** Choutuppal, Ibrahimpatnam, Kandukur, Amangal, Chevella, Shankarpally, Sangareddy.
- **North status:**
  - Land for the northern corridor has nearly all reached final notification (Gadkari, via Swarajya, 30 Jul 2026).
  - The NHAI bid deadline was extended for the 6th time, to 20 Aug (Telangana Today, Aug 2026).
  - Expected operational by end-2029 (Deccan Chronicle).
- **South status:** the DPR is yet to be finalised (Swarajya, Jul 2026), and farmer protests continue (TOI / The South First).

## Land
- **Compensation, north:** highest ₹1.27 cr/acre (land abutting NH-65); lowest ₹16 lakh/acre (Doultabad, Hathnoora mandal) (Deccan Chronicle). Ratio ≈ 7.9×.
- **Market claims:** road-facing land near the alignment is quoted at ₹1.5–2 cr/acre (Hans India). These are real-estate-led figures and are not used in the video.

## Framing rules
- Not investment advice.
- The map route is approximate (towns joined), not the surveyed alignment.
- Viewers are told to check the alignment in government records.

## Correction (user script review)
- **Total length:** 362.5 km = North 161.5 km + South 201 km (Deccan Chronicle; DPR for the 201 km south segment). The "340 km" figure used the old 182 km south arm.
- **North:** six-lane, access-controlled, 161 km (NHAI tender). Land acquisition is complete or nearly complete (Komatireddy, via Deccan Chronicle; Gadkari: 99% notified).
- **South:** alignment awaits the Centre ("RRR South in limbo", Deccan Chronicle). DPR not finalised.
- **HMDA:** the proposed 100 m RRR alignment is incorporated in planning records (G.O.Ms.No.19). Buyers should check the survey number against the official alignment.
