# Petrol ₹100 split — research (2026-10-29)

## Delhi build-up (BPCL, effective 1 Oct 2026, as reported)
- Price to dealer, including Centre's excise: ₹81.12
- Dealer commission: ₹4.41
- Delhi VAT (19.4%): ₹16.59
- Total: ₹102.12
- Excise is ₹11.90 per litre. That is after the cut on 26 Mar 2026, when the special additional excise duty went from ₹13 to ₹3 (Notification 11/2026-CE, per MyCarHelpline).
- So the oil company's share (crude + refining + freight + margin) is about ₹69.2.

## Hyderabad (derived, and it checks out)
- Telangana VAT is 35.2%. Applied to the Delhi pre-VAT price: (81.12 + 4.41) × 1.352 = ₹115.64, against the actual ₹115.69. ✅
- Of ₹115.69: oil company about 69.2, Centre's excise 11.9, dealer 4.4, Telangana VAT about 30.1.
- **Per ₹100 at a Hyderabad pump: about ₹60 oil company, ₹10 Centre, ₹4 dealer, ₹26 State. In total, about ₹36 of every ₹100 is tax.**
- Hyderabad is the costliest metro; Telangana Today wrote on 15 May 2026 that "Hyderabad records highest petrol prices among metros".
- Politics: Harish Rao (BRS) asked CM Revanth Reddy to cut VAT (Telangana Today, 18 Jun 2026). Use this neutrally.
