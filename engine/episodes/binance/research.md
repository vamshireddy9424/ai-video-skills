# Binance heist, 29 Oct 2026: research (as of 31 Oct 2026)

## Headline
- About $6.3bn (Binance, CNBC), $6.36bn (TRM, valued at time of theft), or $6.4bn (Chainalysis). The largest crypto theft on record, about 4× Bybit (Feb 2025, $1.5bn).
- In rupees: at any USD/INR above 80, it is over ₹50,000 crore. The video says "₹50,000 కోట్లకు పైగా" (more than ₹50,000 crore).

## Timeline (TRM, UTC; IST = UTC+5:30)
- **28 Oct, morning:** The operator wallet is funded with three small Tornado Cash withdrawals. TRM says preparation began at least 36 hours before the theft.
- **29 Oct:** Attack code is deployed and tested on two wallets, then rehearsed on a third wallet. About 4 hours later it is installed on a Binance hot wallet.
- **20:05–20:09 UTC (30 Oct, 01:35 IST):** Five XRP wallets send 233.46M XRP.
- **20:20:** The BTC hot wallet sends 18,270 BTC (≈ $1.47bn). The Tron wallet sends 612.7M USDT. On Ethereum, one transaction carries 50 EIP-7702 authorizations, and 3 min 24 s later the whole Ethereum hot wallet (167,464 ETH plus 1.615bn USDT) is sent to the attacker.
- **Overall:** Funds moved across 10 blockchains, mostly within about 20 minutes (CNBC/TRM). Second wave at 23:05 (Avalanche, Polygon).

## Entry point
Binance: the hacker made unauthorised transactions after "gaining access to an engineer's compromised laptop" (Al Jazeera). TRM: signatures valid on 10 chains within about 30 min point to compromise of the hot-wallet signing infrastructure.

## Freezes (about $2.7–3bn)
- Tether ≈ 1.77bn USDT
- Circle ≈ 825M USDC
- Ripple 40.5M RLUSD
- WLF 27.8M USD1
- Arbitrum Security Council ≈ $30M

Freezes began at 5:14 pm ET (Chainalysis). The attacker dumped 325M USDT for ETH at a discount above 10% to outrun the freezes.

## Attribution
TRM ("high confidence") and Chainalysis: North Korea's TraderTraitor (Lazarus-linked), the same group the FBI named for Bybit. The funds commingled with last month's Bitget theft. The pattern matches Bybit and KelpDAO (Apr 2026).

## Now
- The attacker still holds more than 420,000 ETH and 18,270 BTC, about $2.4bn beyond issuer reach (TRM, 30 Oct 00:30 UTC).
- Binance suspended deposits and withdrawals and pledged to cover all losses. Its SAFU fund is about $1.2bn (Fundstrat estimate).
- BTC fell about 5%, below $77k (Al Jazeera).
- Binance AUM is about $155bn. Fundstrat sees no significant solvency risk.
