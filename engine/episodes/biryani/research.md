# ₹300 chicken biryani: where the money goes (Hyderabad, estimate)

This is an illustrative per-plate model for a mid-range Hyderabad restaurant, not one hotel's books. The video says "సగటు hotel లెక్క" (an average hotel's numbers).

| Item | ₹/plate | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Chicken, ~200 g skinless | 52 | Hyderabad skinless reported ₹193 to ₹370/kg across 2026 reports; ₹260/kg used |
| Basmati, ~150 g raw | 17 | ~₹110/kg wholesale |
| Masala, oil, ghee, onion, curd, raita, salan | 25 | estimate |
| Gas | 8 | 19 kg commercial LPG ≈ ₹3,065 in Hyderabad after the 1 Oct 2026 revision (≈ ₹161/kg), ~50 g per plate in batch dum |
| **Food + gas** | **102** | 34% of price; industry norm is 28–35% food cost |
| Staff salaries | 50 | estimate |
| Rent, power, water | 50 | estimate |
| Packing, wastage, maintenance, marketing | 44 | estimate |
| GST 5% (inside ₹300) | 14 | restaurant GST 5% |
| **Profit** | **40** | ≈13%; NRAI-cited average margins are 5–12%, brands claim 20–30% |

## Same plate on a delivery app
- Commission 25% of the food value (₹286) = ₹71.5, plus 18% GST on the commission = ₹12.9, so ≈ ₹85 to the app. Reported 2026 commissions are 20–30%, with GST on top.
- Hotel: ₹40 − ₹85 ≈ **−₹45** per plate at the same ₹300 price.
- Break-even on the app: payout 0.705 × (P / 1.05) ≥ ₹246 gives P ≈ ₹366, so the app price has to cross about ₹360 just to avoid a loss.

Sources: startuptalky / Moneycontrol-cited commission 24–30% (2026); goodreturns LPG 16 Oct 2026; Siasat / Telangana Today chicken rates; dineopen and registerkaro food-cost norms. All rough; labelled as an estimate in the video.
